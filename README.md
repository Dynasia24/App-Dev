# My Favorite Movie: Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

## Overview
**Captain America: The Winter Soldier** is a superhero spy-thriller directed by **Anthony and Joe Russo**. Set in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, the film follows **Steve Rogers** as he adapts to life in the modern world while working for S.H.I.E.L.D., the global counter-terrorism agency.

When a high-ranking S.H.I.E.L.D. official is targeted, Steve uncovers a massive conspiracy embedded within the organization. Teaming up with Black Widow and the Falcon, Captain America faces a dangerous new threat: a ghost-like assassin known only as the **Winter Soldier**.

---

## Main Characters

### 1. Steve Rogers / Captain America
- **Actor:** Chris Evans
- **Role:** Leader & Super Soldier
- **Description:** A World War II hero fighting to uphold his moral compass while navigating modern political espionage and betrayal within S.H.I.E.L.D.

### 2. Bucky Barnes / The Winter Soldier
- **Actor:** Sebastian Stan
- **Role:** Central Antagonist / Assassin
- **Description:** Steve's childhood best friend, presumed dead during WWII, who was brainwashed and cybernetically enhanced by HYDRA to become an elite assassin.

### 3. Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow
- **Actor:** Scarlett Johansson
- **Role:** Master Spy & Combatant
- **Description:** A highly skilled S.H.I.E.L.D. agent who works closely with Steve Rogers to expose hidden corruption while confronting her own dark past.

### 4. Sam Wilson / Falcon
- **Actor:** Anthony Mackie
- **Role:** Tactical Ally
- **Description:** A former Air Force pararescueman equipped with a specialized flight harness, who quickly becomes one of Captain America's most trusted allies.

### 5. Nick Fury
- **Actor:** Samuel L. Jackson
- **Role:** Director of S.H.I.E.L.D.
- **Description:** The secretive head of S.H.I.E.L.D. whose investigation into internal compromises sets off the central conflict of the film.
